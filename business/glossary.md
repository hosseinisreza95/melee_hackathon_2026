# Glossary
- **PPP:** Plant protection product.
- **Authorized use:** Crop, target, dose, application and restrictions linked to a registration.
- **Original value:** Exact source wording.
- **Normalized value:** Harmonized value for comparison.
- **EPPO code:** Harmonized identifier maintained by EPPO.
- **Sister-product candidate:** Reviewable hypothesis, not confirmed identity.
- **Validation status:** Review state, separate from model confidence.
- **Frozen extract:** Dated copy supplied when live access is unreliable or restricted.
