# Mapping files

- EPPO crop and pest rows are starter candidates and remain `to_verify` until checked through EPPO Data Services.
- `confidence_score` reflects confidence in a proposed relationship, not regulatory validity.
- `review_status=validated` is reserved for relationships explicitly supported by LPC source material.
- Sister-product candidates must not be presented as confirmed equivalents.
- Strain identity is more specific than species identity and must be preserved.
